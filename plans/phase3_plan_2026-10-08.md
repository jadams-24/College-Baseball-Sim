# Phase 3 plan: handedness and platoon splits (for owner approval, 2026-10-08)

Starts after PR B merges. Data: `data/ncaa_2025/roster_aggregates/` (fetch of 2026-10-08, 232 of 277 D1 teams, approved as representative without weighting) and the 2025 WMT play-by-play.

## Design rules
- No code path reads tier to set handedness (owner rule 2026-10-08). Hands are drawn per player, conditional on talent and role (pitchers) or position (batters). The tier gradient (LHP share P4 .35 → low .20) must emerge from talent concentrating at P4. It is gated as a check, not fitted.
- Platoon effects are league-level matchup shifts on top of the Phase 2 odds-ratio model, centred so league totals do not move.
- Everything else as in the earlier phases: rates and models from data, constants in `config/`, guesses in GUESSES.md.

## 1. Data: one more aggregate pass (no raw data kept)
The committed tables have handedness by role, position and tier, but not by talent. A talent-conditional model needs that.
1. **Hand x performance bins** (new aggregator table, counts only).
   - Matched pitchers: throws by role (starter / reliever) x quintile of opponent-adjusted K−BB% per BF.
   - Matched batters: bats (L/R/S) x throws x position group x quintile of opponent-adjusted on-base and power rate per PA.
   - Each cell also records its mean trials, so measurement noise can be deconvolved in the fit.
2. **Relief and pinch-hit usage by hand** (new table, counts only). Relief entries and pinch hitters by the hands of the batter due up and of the pitcher. This is the data for the lefty-specialist hook the Phase 6 bullpen Decider keeps, and for platoon pinch hitting. Both are AI decisions fitted from data, the same as in PR B.
3. Same leak check; one workflow run (about 3½ hours). The existing tables are kept.

## 2. Model
1. **Throwing hand** by position group: P(throws L | position).
   - From `handedness_by_position`: catchers and middle infielders throw left almost never; first basemen, outfielders and pitchers do.
   - For pitchers, P(throws L | role, talent) is a logistic on the drawn pitcher's talent index (his K−BB logit offset) and role. It is fitted to the hand x quintile table through the known noise of the observed quintiles (the Phase 2 talent distribution maps true talent to observed-bin probabilities).
2. **Batting side** given throws, position group and talent: multinomial L / R / S. The fit is the same, through the noise of the observed quintiles.
3. **Switch hitters** bat from the side opposite the pitcher. The share of their plate appearances from each side (`platoon_league`, basis side_used) is a check, not a parameter.
4. **Platoon shifts.**
   - Same-hand vs opposite-hand logit shifts for K, BB, HBP, HR, BABIP and extra-base share, separately for left and right batters.
   - Fitted from `platoon_league` (73,393 plate appearances with both hands known, tier-reweighted to the full-season matchup mix). Each cell is compared with the Phase 2 matchup model's prediction for the same plate appearances, so the shift is net of who faced whom.
   - Each shift is centred on the league handedness mix, so league rates stay unchanged.
5. **Individual platoon spread.**
   - `platoon_spread` has 86 batters with 50+ plate appearances against each hand. True spread is not detectable at that size (true_sd net of the permutation null ≈ 0.18 logit for K, noise-dominated).
   - Default: league-level shifts only, with the individual spread reported as a check.
   - If the bigger aggregate pass (20+ plate appearances against each hand, hierarchical moments) detects a spread, it is drawn per player. Otherwise it is listed in GUESSES.md as zero.
6. **AI manager.** Relief choice and pinch hitting by hand, fitted from the new usage table (the lefty-specialist hook from Phase 6). The decisions keep equal odds for human and AI.

## 3. Gate (40 seasons; same tolerance rules)
1. Every Phase 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 row, and PR B's rows, unchanged on the same run (league totals unchanged).
2. Handedness shares, overall, against `roster_representativeness.md`:
   - LHP share among starters and relievers;
   - batters' L / R / S by position group;
   - throws by position group.
3. **Tier gradient check:** LHP share by tier (P4 .35 / .31, mid .26 / .25, low .21 / .20 for starters / relievers) and batters' L share by tier. Not fitted, so it tests the talent-conditional draw. Tolerance from the clustered intervals.
4. **Platoon splits:** league K%, BB%, HR%, BABIP and OBP for the four hand pairs (L-L, L-R, R-L, R-R) and switch hitters, sim against `platoon_league`. Plus the share of plate appearances with the platoon advantage, which tests lineups and bullpens.
5. **Usage by hand:** relief entries against left-handed batters due up, and pinch hits for the platoon advantage, sim against the new usage table.
6. Reported, not gated: the qualified-player platoon spread (real estimate too noisy to gate) and coverage notes (41% of real plate appearances have both hands known; low tier 27%).
