# Phase 8 — roster rules: kickoff plan (2026-11-26)

Status: queued by the owner on 2026-11-26. Nothing is built yet. The design spec
(`design/phase9_recruiting.md`, Section 13, "Phase 8: roster rules (the container)") is the source of
truth; this file is the order of work, what was checked before starting, and the decisions the owner
still has to make.

## What Phase 8 is

Rosters stop being a one-time draw and become a container that carries players from year to year.
No recruiting behaviour yet: each year's newcomers come from the existing talent draw, and every
returning player is one year older.

Scope (from the spec):

- the 34-man roster limit, and roster composition by class, position and handedness;
- the eligibility clock (the age-based five-year clock from 8/1/26: no redshirts, no seasons cap,
  JUCO time counts; spec Section 14, item 14) and the class year shown on every screen;
- injuries and medical exemptions where the rules provide them;
- the money pools and cap accounting (scholarship %, revenue share, NIL as a separate collective);
- cuts (the user's, with AI suggestions; AI programs automatic);
- the rule side of player flow: portal windows and exceptions, draft eligibility, JUCO and D2
  transfer rules.

## Checked before starting (2026-11-26)

1. **Handedness.** Three worlds (seeds 7, 642244, 1; 921 teams): batters are 60% R/R, 21–23% L/R,
   13% L/L, 4% switch; no team is all right-handed (the lowest team share of R/R is .18–.29, the
   highest .94). The engine's draw is sound. The owner's screenshot of an all-R/R Central Michigan
   roster was not reproduced: that dynasty's seed is needed to look further.
2. **Bench batters share ratings.** On every team, all eight bench batters have the same Contact and
   the same Gap rating, and nearly the same Power. Cause: the fitted individual spread for bench
   batters (`cfg.talent`, method of moments on small samples) is 0 for BABIP and for the extra-base
   share, and .089 for HR, against .098, .225 and .456 for regulars. It is a data limit, not a bug,
   and harmless to the season's statistics (bench batters bat little). **It matters from Phase 8 on:**
   a bench freshman is a future regular, so players need individual talent whatever their role.
   Requirement for Phase 8: draw every position player's talent from one roster-wide distribution and
   let the role (regular or bench) follow from talent and class, with the regular and bench rate
   distributions reproduced as a gate row.
3. **Roster size.** The engine already carries 17 position players and 18 pitchers (35) per team,
   the players who actually appear in a 2025 season. The 34-man limit is therefore close to the
   playing roster; Phase 8 decides whether non-playing depth exists.
4. **Arm rating.** Only catchers and outfielders have one (the data isolates nothing for
   infielders). Recruiting will want an infield arm; that is a GUESS to be marked when Phase 9
   needs it, not part of Phase 8.

## Order of work

Each step ends with the realism report and the gate rows it can move.

1. **Data pull for the gate.** Class composition by school and tier from the committed roster
   aggregates (`data/ncaa_2025/roster_aggregates/origins_by_school.csv`, class x origin); roster
   size by team. No new fetch expected.
2. **One talent distribution per position player** (item 2 above), with role assignment from talent
   and class. Gate: every Phase 2–7 row unchanged; regular and bench rate distributions reproduced.
3. **Age, clock and class year.** Each player gets an age and years left on the five-year clock;
   the class year replaces the dash on the roster, lineup and player pages.
4. **The year loop.** After Omaha: players leave when the clock runs out (the draft and the portal
   are rules only in this phase: eligibility dates and windows, no behaviour), newcomers are drawn
   to refill, everyone ages one year. Year 2 becomes playable in the dynasty.
5. **The 34-man limit and cuts.** The deadline, the user's cuts with AI suggestions, AI programs'
   automatic cuts.
6. **Money pools and cap accounting** as bookkeeping (no offers yet).
7. **The gate.** Roster size and class composition per team against the 2025 rosters; origin shares
   by tier; every Phase 1–7 row unchanged over 20+ simulated years of turnover against the
   drawn-roster baseline, with the talent and team-strength distribution by tier matching the
   scoreboard decomposition each year.

## Decisions for the owner before the build

1. **Development in Phase 8.** With no development model until Phase 10, a returning player keeps
   his ratings as he ages. Accept that for Phase 8 (the gate checks the distribution, not growth),
   or pull a simple, data-fitted age curve forward?
2. **Non-playing depth.** Keep rosters at the 35 who play, or add players who never appear so that
   cuts and the 34-man limit have something to bind on?
3. **Money pools now or with recruiting.** The spec puts cap accounting in Phase 8; it does nothing
   visible until offers exist in Phase 9. Build it now, or move it to the start of Phase 9?
4. **Injuries.** The spec lists them; the engine has no injury model. In Phase 8, or deferred?

## Related, not part of Phase 8
