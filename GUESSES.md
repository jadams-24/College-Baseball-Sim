# Guesses

Constants in `config/` or `engine/` that cannot be traced to a value in
`benchmarks.json` or to a source in `PHASE0_NOTES.md`. Each entry: where, what,
why it is a guess, and what would replace it. Everything else the engine uses is
a rate read from `benchmarks.json` or from `data/ncaa_2025/derived/engine_tables_2025.json`.

| Where | Constant | Value | Why a guess | Replace with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `config/phase1.py` | `MIN_CELL_N` | 30 | Minimum observations before a joint advancement cell is used instead of its fallback (pooled over outs, then independent per-runner marginals). A statistical threshold, not a baseball rate. | A proper shrinkage estimator across cells |
| `config/phase1.py` | `run_rule_margin`, `run_rule_after_inning` | 10, 7 | Taken from the *text* of `game_structure.run_rule` ("commonly 10 runs after 7 innings", conf C). Conferences differ; whether a given game is under the rule is drawn from the empirical share of 10-run games that ended early (`run_rule_freq.p_ended_early_given_margin_10plus_wmt`). | Per-conference rule table in Phase 7 |
| `config/phase1.py` | `extra_innings_placed_runner` | False | `game_structure.extra_innings_tiebreaker` says the runner-on-second rule is conference-optional and not universal; plain extra innings are used. Affects extra-inning length, not frequency. | Per-conference rule table in Phase 7 |
| `engine/game.py` | runner-collision rule | trailing runner stops one base short, or is out if that base is also taken | Only reachable through the independent per-runner marginal fallback (sparse cells); the joint tables never produce collisions. The run reports how often it fired. | Larger sample so no cell is sparse |
| `engine/game.py` | one base-running event per plate appearance | — | Steal attempts, wild pitches, passed balls, pickoffs and balks are drawn at most once before each PA at the empirical per-PA rate for the state. Real innings occasionally have two such events before one PA; the per-PA rate already counts them, so totals match but a few events land on the next PA. | Sequential event draws with a per-event hazard |
| `config/phase2.py` | `MIN_HAZARD_N` | 30 | Batters faced needed before a pull-hazard cell is used instead of its coarser backoff. Statistical threshold. | Smoothed hazard model |
| `engine/manager.py` | pull beyond observed range | pull at 120+ pitches | Only reached when no hazard cell has data (pitch counts past the sample's maximum). | — |
| `engine/manager.py` | batting order | talent order (expected OBP + SLG, best first) | Manager behavior; affects how PA are spread across the nine starters. | Manager AI in Phase 6 |
| `engine/game2.py` | no in-game substitutions | — | Starters bat all game; no pinch hitters or defensive changes, so fewer batters reach the 75%-of-games qualification line (6.6 vs 7.6 per team). | Manager AI in Phase 6 |
| `scripts/build_phase2_benchmarks.py` | `FULL_SEASON_GAMES`, `MIN_TEAM_GAMES`, `MIN_TRIALS` | 40, 25, 30 | Sample-selection thresholds for the variance estimates. Statistical choices. | — |
| `config/phase2.py` | `ROTATION_STAFF` | ranks 1-3 weekend starters, 4-5 midweek starters, 6-8 top relievers | The data give how often a team's k-th most frequent weekend starter starts (and whole-series patterns), not which role that pitcher holds otherwise. | Swingman role from play-by-play appearance sequences |
| `engine/league.py` | rotation and bullpen ranks | ordered by K − BB − HR logit offsets | Rank 1 is the best pitcher by strikeout, walk and home run talent; real managers order by results, which correlate but are not the same. | Manager AI in Phase 6 |
| `engine/manager.py` | starters never relieve; series always Fri-Sun | — | Real top-three pitchers throw 10-15 IP a season in starts outside Fri-Sun series (Thursday openers, tournaments) and the third-busiest throws 19 of his 54 IP in relief. Too few pitchers reach 50 IP and the qualified group is ace-heavy (see reports/phase2.md). | Swingman usage and Thursday series from the play-by-play; tournaments in Phase 7 |
| `engine/league.py` | one talent scale | team (o, d) moves rates only along the P4-minus-low tier contrast (`v_bat`, `v_pit`) | Assumes within-tier team quality differs in the same rate mix as tiers do. The rest of the play-by-play team variance is kept as style with zero run value. | All-D1 team rate tables |
| `engine/league.py` | team style correlation | individual correlation matrix | Team style offsets use the individual correlation; 50 full-season teams are too few to estimate a separate one. | All-D1 team tables |
| `engine/league.py` | independents | each draws its own conference effect from its tier's conference distribution | One P4 independent; it has no conference to share an effect with. | — |
| `engine/game2.py` | home edge split | half on the home batters (batting direction), half on the home pitchers (pitching direction) | The scoreboard identifies the total matchup-controlled home effect, not its batting/pitching split. | Home/away splits from all-D1 team tables |
| `engine/game2.py` | earned runs on error plays | every run scoring on a play with an error or passed ball is unearned | The inning is reconstructed with phantom outs, but runner advancement is not re-run without the error, so a run that would have scored anyway is counted unearned. Sim earned share .8824 matches the data's .8824. | Re-run the advancement draw without the error |
| `engine/schedule.py` | hosting strength | the team-level (o, d) draw, before its players are drawn | The hosting slope was fitted on the scoreboard's estimated strengths; the players' realized share is small (SD .05 log runs). | — |
| `config/phase4.py` | rating maps | each rating linear in one true rate's logit offset; batter HBP and pitcher HBP, BABIP and XBH allowed unrated (hidden) | The spec maps each rating to one or more rates; one rate per rating keeps the round trip identified. Pitcher BABIP and XBH allowed are team defense in this engine and are rated with fielding. | Fielding ratings in Phase 6 |
| `config/phase4.py` | `RESERVED` speed | no Speed rating | The engine's base running is league tables with no player term; a Speed rating would map to nothing. | Speed-linked rates (SB, extra bases taken) in Phase 6 |
| `engine/league.py` | ratings are normal | each rating's true distribution is the Phase 2 normal (logit) distribution | The Phase 2 talent model is Gaussian on the logit scale; the tails are not measured separately. Same Gaussian-tail caveat as the CLAUDE.md watch item. | All-D1 player tables |
| `config/phase4.py` | stamina log-normal | log theta ~ N(mu, sd^2) by role, proportional-hazards multiplier on the pull hazard | Functional form for the individual leash. mu and sd are fitted (`stamina_2025`); the form is assumed. The method-of-moments check agrees (log SD .52 vs .54 starters, .51 vs .62 relievers). | Nonparametric prior from more seasons |
| `config/phase4.py` | `MIN_TRIALS` | 150 PA / 100 BIP / 35 hits / 150 BF / 8 apps | Identification threshold for the round-trip statistics (every player is still estimated). Statistical choice. | — |
| `config/phase4.py` | `SHARED_TAU_ROLES` | bench | Bench players' prior SD is shared across tiers in the estimator (only the mean differs by tier): their few trials leave a per-tier SD unidentified. Statistical choice. | — |
| `config/phase4.py` | `FOLD_SEASONS`, EB grid and search settings | 2 seasons per fold; 401-point grid ±4; 20 golden steps | Numerical and replication settings of the estimator. | — |
| `engine/pitch.py` | pitch chain state | the next pitch depends only on the count and the matchup | No pitch-count fatigue, times-through-order or sequencing effects within a PA beyond the count; the data has no pitch type or location to build them on. | Pitch-level data with type and location; fatigue in Phase 6 |
| `engine/pitch.py` | player directions | linear in the K and BB logit offsets, the same at every count | Split-half slopes are measured on whole-season rates; how a player's tilt varies by count is not identified at these sample sizes. HBP has its own tilt only (too rare for a direction). | More seasons of sequences |
| `engine/pitch.py` | neutral pitch N | a mid-sequence P (feed coding) is a pitch that changes nothing, at its per-count rate | The feed counts it in the pitch count without a call; what it was is not recorded. | — |
| `engine/game2.py` | intentional walks | get a full four-ball sequence like any walk | Phase 2 folds IBB into BB; real IBBs are mostly automatic (no pitches). About 0.2% of PAs. | IBB through the Decider in Phase 6 |
